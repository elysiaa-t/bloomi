---
name: student-voice
description: Use when writing any text a student will read or hear,
  including instructions, feedback, hints, and the AI partner's
  dialogue. Not for the adult report.
---
- Short sentences, common words, at or below the student's grade
  level. One instruction at a time.
- Feedback is specific and starts with what worked: "You read 'team'
  and 'seat' perfectly. Let's look at 'bread' together."
- Never use "wrong", "incorrect", "failed", "easy", or "simple".
- After a mistake, offer one hint, then show the answer with a short
  reason. Never make the student guess more than twice.
- Praise effort and strategy, not being smart.
- Calm and warm. No exclamation-mark overload, no baby talk, no
  sarcasm.
- When the AI plays a confused classmate in math, it asks real
  questions and thanks the student for the explanation.
- The student can always ask a question without it being scored.
